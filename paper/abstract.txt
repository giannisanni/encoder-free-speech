Most speech-capable language models attach a separate audio encoder to a (frozen or fine-tuned) text LLM. We ask a narrower question: is a frozen multimodal LLM's native, encoder-free audio pathway sufficient, and can lightweight adapters alone give it a voice? We couple a frozen 4-bit Gemma-4 backbone with a CSM-style speech-output head (a semantic-codebook predictor plus a depth transformer over Mimi residual-vector-quantized codes) and train only QLoRA adapters and the head on a single 16 GB GPU. On the smaller E2B backbone this recipe teaches a fully frozen, quantized LLM to speak: a text-to-speech model reaches 13% word error rate and 88% word recall on held-out sentences (RQ2). For the encoder-free 12B backbone (RQ1) we report three findings about how audio reaches a frozen multimodal LLM. First, naive 4-bit NF4 quantization silently destroys the backbone's audio comprehension: the quantized model transcribes nothing yet still conditions on prosody, and keeping only the small audio projector and tower in bf16 (a roughly 10.5 GB "hybrid" load) restores verbatim understanding. Second, the delivery mechanism matters: the backbone's native multimodal pathway conveys lexical content, whereas a hand-built feature-splice of the same audio features conveys prosody but not content. Third, exploiting these, a two-stage cascade (native audio understanding into the frozen backbone, then the CSM-style speech head voicing the reply) yields conversational speech-to-speech with no backbone training, holding relevant spoken exchanges and, after a short head-refinement run, rendering clean multi-sentence (roughly 13 to 20 second) replies with correct word onsets and out-of-distribution generalization. Two methodological findings recur throughout: teacher-forced codebook cross-entropy is not a proxy for free-running intelligibility (a memorizing model scores well yet produces 141% WER, and the same trap reappears for comprehension), so word error rate on decoded audio is the necessary gate; and the Mimi codec imposes an entropy floor that cross-entropy training cannot cross, capping naturalness and motivating a flow-matching acoustic head. A single end-to-end model using native delivery is the remaining step.
